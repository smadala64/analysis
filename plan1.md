The Member examples tab is good. The Provider examples tab doesn’t meet the standard and needs redoing:

chat_version: every provider row says “Why was our claim denied?”. Rewrite each one from its own caller_question and summary, keeping the specifics (procedure, reason hint, situation), e.g. “Why was our deep cleaning claim denied for [patient] — what documentation do you need?” Vary the style as with members.
chat_response: the current responses are templates (“I can check [denial reason] and [next step]…”) with no facts from the summary. Rewrite each one individually, like the member tab: state the actual reason or status from the conversation_summary (e.g. denied for missing periodontal charting, other insurance on file as primary, frequency limit reached), and give the office the concrete next step (resubmit with X, update COB, file a reconsideration with Y). Use office-appropriate wording (“the claim for your patient…”).
bot_handling and bot_data_needed: assess each row individually. Not every case is “full”.
Brackets, both tabs: use [ ] only for values the bot would look up (date of service, amount, procedure, reason code, deadline), not around labels like “[root canal claim]” or “[procedure denial]”. Fix this on the member tab too.
