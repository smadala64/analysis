Run the same drill-down as Find a dentist for Billing and premiums → Pay premium or manage autopay. Method:

Read the full clean transcript for each question, in batches of 100, with checkpointing. Decide each item yourself. No keyword rules or pattern matching.
Step 1: confirm it belongs. This subcategory is for making a premium payment or managing how premiums are paid. Move a question out if it’s really about:
how much the premium or invoice is, or why it changed → Premium or invoice amount
enrolling, cancelling or changing the plan → Enroll, cancel or change plan
coverage being active or terminated (with no payment action) → Eligibility
claims or dentist bills (not premiums) → the correct Claims subcategory
anything else → its correct subcategory
Exclude moved questions. Report how many moved and where they went.
Step 2: for confirmed questions only, record:
What they wanted to do: make a one-time payment; set up autopay; change or cancel autopay; update card or bank details; check whether a payment was received or posted; resolve a failed, late or missed payment; ask about a refund or overpayment; find out how or where to pay (online, phone, mail address); change the billing date; other (describe briefly)
Why they called (the main reason): couldn’t pay online or the website/app didn’t work; autopay failed or charged incorrectly; received a past-due or termination notice; new member making a first payment; changed bank or card; didn’t know how to pay; prefers to pay by phone; other (describe briefly)
How the agent helped: took the payment on the call; set up or changed autopay; explained how to pay online; gave the mailing address or phone payment line; transferred to billing; couldn’t help
Coverage at risk? Whether the caller mentioned past due, a grace period, termination or reinstatement (yes/no)
Plan context (if stated): individual, employer group, Medicare or government program
Outcome: resolved on the call, or follow-up needed
Caller type: member or other
Quality check at the end: re-read 50 random confirmed questions blind and report agreement in chat.
