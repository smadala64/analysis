Prompt: General plan or coverage information, full review

Run the same drill-down as Find a dentist for Benefits and coverage → General plan or coverage information (~2,278 questions). Review every question and report exact counts, not estimates.

Method:

Read the full clean transcript for each question, in batches of 100, with checkpointing so the work can resume. Decide each item yourself. No keyword rules or pattern matching.
Step 1: confirm it belongs. This subcategory is for general questions about the plan or coverage, not about a specific procedure, limit or balance. Using the existing taxonomy definitions, move a question out if it’s really about:
a specific procedure → Procedure coverage
maximum, deductible or remaining benefits → Maximum, deductible or benefit balance
frequency, waiting period or replacement limits → Frequency, waiting or replacement limit
whether coverage is active, or effective/termination dates → Eligibility
anything else → its correct subcategory
Exclude moved questions from the analysis. Report how many moved and where they went.
Step 2: for confirmed questions only, record:
What they wanted to know: what the plan covers in general, coverage percentages by service type (preventive/basic/major), plan type or name, network type (PPO/Premier/DHMO/Medicaid…), plan year or renewal, who is covered (dependents), whether a benefit exists at all (e.g. orthodontics), a benefits summary or document, other (describe briefly)
Why they called (the main reason): new member, plan changed or open enrollment, planning upcoming treatment, couldn’t find or understand the info online, received a document they didn’t understand, employer or group question, provider verifying a patient’s benefits, other (describe briefly)
How the agent helped: explained over the phone, sent a benefits summary or document, directed to website or app, transferred, couldn’t help
Plan or group context (if stated): plan type, employer group vs individual, government program
Outcome: resolved on the call, or follow-up needed
Caller type: member or provider
Quality check at the end: re-read 50 random confirmed questions blind and report agreement in chat.

Output: ./output/General_Plan_Info_Simple.xlsx, same 4 tabs, exact counts:

Summary: the total reviewed; how many were confirmed and how many were moved out (and to which subcategories); then 5–7 plain-English findings and 2–3 “What this means” bullets (e.g. which questions the member portal, a benefits summary or the voice bot could answer).
Top reasons: confirmed questions only. Columns: Reason | Questions | % | What they wanted to know most | Resolved on call % | Example
What they needed: what they wanted to know, and how the agent helped, with counts and %. Show member and provider separately if they differ.
Data to capture: data element | why useful | how often it was identifiable.

Rules: same simple style as Find_A_Dentist_Simple.xlsx. Plain tables, plain English, no charts, extra tabs, technical terms or template-like examples. Examples must be real de-identified summaries.

Reply in chat with: the confirmed vs moved-out counts and where they went, the summary bullets, and the blind-recheck agreement.
