Method:

Read the full clean transcript for each question, in batches of 100, with checkpointing. Decide each item yourself. No keyword rules or pattern matching.
Step 1: confirm it belongs. This subcategory is for getting, replacing, correcting or using an ID card or member/group number. Move a question out if it’s really about:
updating address, name, contact details or other account info (with no card request) → Account maintenance or correspondence
other plan documents or forms (benefits booklet, claim form, etc.) → Plan document or form
whether coverage is active or effective dates → Eligibility
enrolling, cancelling or changing the plan → Enroll, cancel or change plan
anything else → its correct subcategory

Step 2: for confirmed questions only, record:
What they wanted: a new or replacement physical card; a digital or temporary card (email, fax, app); their member ID or group number given verbally; a card for a dependent; a correction to a card (wrong name, missing dependent, wrong plan); to understand what’s on the card; other (describe briefly)
Why they called (the main reason): never received a card after enrolling; lost or damaged card; new plan or plan year; name or dependent change; dentist’s office asked for the card or ID; couldn’t access or use the app or website; didn’t know a digital card was available; other (describe briefly)
Urgency: whether the caller mentioned an appointment today or soon (yes/no)
How the agent helped: gave the ID or group number on the call; emailed or faxed a temporary card; ordered a replacement card; directed to the app or website to download it; corrected or updated details; transferred; couldn’t help
Who called: member, family member, or dentist’s office/provider
Outcome: resolved on the call, or follow-up needed
Quality check at the end: re-read 50 random confirmed questions blind and report agreement in chat.
